## 1. Brand foundation

### Brand essence — "Own the Outcome"

Quantum Coast Capital builds the ecosystem from which returns emerge. Emerging
markets are not found; they are built. The firm develops state partnerships,
physical infrastructure, academic pipelines, and hardware relationships that
produce proprietary deal flow and de-risked entry points.

> The source voice document describes "Own the Outcome" as the brand essence and
> states it is *"not a tagline or slogan for use in materials"* — then a later
> section labels it "Tagline." The live site currently uses it as a closing
> statement on the Home and About pages.
> **Until this is resolved, treat it as the brand essence and internal shorthand.
> Do not introduce it as a lockup, signature, or slogan in new materials without
> written approval.**

### Brand archetype — The Architect

Motivated by the conviction that emerging markets are built, not discovered.
Sees the ecosystem before the deal, and constructs the infrastructure,
relationships, and standards that make a category investable.

- Finds advantage through execution before consensus, technical fluency, and the
  discipline to build what others wait to buy.
- Operates as an **operator, not an allocator** — entrepreneurial execution plus
  institutional finance discipline plus scientific understanding.
- Balances long-horizon conviction with the daily work of compounding structural
  advantage.

**Primary shape:** Arch / horizon line
**Visual metaphor:** Interlocking system / coastal horizon

### Value proposition

Build the ecosystem infrastructure that turns quantum breakthroughs into
investable, enduring companies.

### Distinguishing beliefs

- **Ecosystem is the point of contact.** In emerging markets it is the moment of
  consequence where capital, science, and commercialization either align or
  fracture — determining which companies endure.
- **Better infrastructure makes better companies, which make a better world.**
  QCC is a bridge between scientific discovery and the real economy.
- **Success requires perpetual execution.**

### Positioning statement

Quantum Coast Capital is an early-stage venture firm investing in the quantum
economy — computing, sensing, networking, and post-quantum security. It pairs
capital with in-house scientific diligence and works alongside founders through
commercialization and the path to commercial scale.

### Investment focus areas

## 2. Verbal identity

### Voice attributes

| Attribute | Means | Does not mean |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| **Precise** | Specifics, figures, named sources | Vague superlatives |
| **Measured** | Confident, unhurried, declarative | Hype, urgency, breathlessness |
| **Technically fluent** | Comfortable with the science, explains it plainly | Jargon as decoration |
| **Institutional** | Reads like a serious financial firm | Cold, bureaucratic, or corporate-generic |
| **Accountable** | Owns outcomes; operator language | Passive, hedging, distant |

**Reference tone:** Citadel or Carlyle in structure and restraint; warmer and more
editorial in the serif voice. Institutional without being coldly technical.

### Naming conventions

- **"Quantum Coast Capital"** on first reference; **"QCC"** thereafter.
- Legal entity: **Quantum Coast Capital Management LLC** (use in legal, copyright,
  and contractual contexts only).
- Never: "QuantumCoast", "Quantum Coast", "Q.C.C.", "QCC Capital", "Quantum Coast
  Ventures".
- Describe as an **"early-stage venture firm"** — never "fund manager", "private
  equity firm", "hedge fund", or "asset manager".
- Titles: "Founder & Managing Partner" (Matt Cimaglia), "Chief Investment Officer
  & General Partner" (Hillary Matchett), "Senior Advisor" (advisory bench).

### Write this way

- Lead with the claim, then the evidence.
- Plain, declarative sentences. Short paragraphs.
- Spell out technical terms on first use.
- Attribute every market figure to its source **with a date** (e.g. "McKinsey,
  *Quantum Technology Monitor 2026*").
- Use "we" for the firm; avoid third-person self-reference in owned channels.
- Em dashes for asides; serial comma; sentence case for headings.

### Not this way

- ❌ Performance, return, or fundraising claims of any kind (see §3).
- ❌ "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "unlock the future", "the next big thing".
- ❌ Implying quantum replaces classical computing or AI — it works **alongside** them.
- ❌ Predicting specific timelines for technical breakthroughs.
- ❌ Naming portfolio companies or partners without written confirmation they are public.
- ❌ Stock-photo futurism language: "harnessing the power of", "at the forefront of".

### Approved boilerplate

Use verbatim in press releases and partner materials.

> Quantum Coast Capital is an early-stage venture firm investing in the quantum
> economy — quantum computing, sensing, networking, and post-quantum security.
> Based in Palm Beach County, Florida, the firm pairs capital with in-house
> scientific diligence, and works alongside founders through commercialization
> and the path to commercial scale.

**Short form (one sentence):**

> Quantum Coast Capital is an early-stage venture firm investing in the quantum
> economy, based in Palm Beach County, Florida.

### Approved third-party market figures

Attribute inline, every time. Source: McKinsey & Company, *Quantum Technology
Monitor 2026*.

| Figure | Claim |
|--------|-------|
| $2.7T | Economic value quantum computing could create worldwide by 2035 |
| $12.6B | Global investment into quantum companies in 2025 (6.3× year over year) |
| $4.4B | Projected worldwide quantum computing revenue by 2028 (from >$1B in 2025) |
| 300+ | Global enterprises working with quantum vendors, from Airbus to JPMorgan Chase |

Always pair with: *"Figures are McKinsey estimates and projections; forward-looking
figures are not guarantees of future results."*

> **Do not** present these as QCC's figures. They describe the market QCC invests
> into. Phrase as "global", "worldwide", or "industry-wide".

## 3. Compliance guardrails

**Applies to every public asset — web, social, print, deck, email, video.**

QCC is a registered investment firm operating under U.S. securities regulation.
Marketing materials must never constitute an offer or general solicitation.

### Absolute prohibitions

1. **No fundraising language.** Never state or imply that a fund is raising, open,
   or accepting capital. No "invest with us", "now raising", "LP inquiries", or
   capital-commitment calls to action.
2. **No performance claims.** No returns, IRR, multiples, valuations, or
   track-record figures — including for individual investments.
3. **No forward-looking claims about QCC.** Third-party market projections are
   fine when attributed; QCC's own projections are not.
4. **No testimonials or endorsements** from investors regarding the firm.
5. **No fabricated content.** Never invent portfolio companies, fund names,
   documents, or activity — including in mockups, demos, or placeholder copy.

### Required elements

- The standard investment disclaimer appears in the site footer on every page.
  Any standalone material (deck, one-pager, landing page) needs equivalent
  language.
- Third-party figures require inline source attribution and date.

### Standing editorial rules

- **Never reference Dmitry Green** on any QCC property — site, deck, news, bios,
  or social.
- Matt Cimaglia's outside advisory engagements are disclosed by him personally
  and must not be added to QCC materials.
- Keep all firm materials vendor-neutral; do not favor specific hardware vendors.

> **When in doubt, route it to counsel before publishing.** Compliance review is
> cheaper than a correction.

| **Primary logo** | Default. Light and white backgrounds. | [`QCC Logo.svg`](https://r2.quantumcoastcapital.com/QCC%20Logo.svg) |

| **Lettermark (QCC)** | Favicons, avatars, constrained spaces where the full logo is illegible. | [`QCC Icon Black.svg`](https://r2.quantumcoastcapital.com/QCC%20Icon%20Black.svg) |
| **Lettermark, reversed** | Lettermark on dark grounds. | [`qcc-mark-white.svg`](https://r2.quantumcoastcapital.com/brand/ec218295f9165e10981885f5.svg) |

All marks are **SVG** — they scale to any size without loss. Use the supplied
files as-is. Never recreate, redraw, trace, or substitute the wordmark.

### Clear space

Maintain clear space on all sides equal to the **height of the "Q"** in the
wordmark. Nothing — type, imagery, rules, or edges — enters that zone.

### Minimum size

| Context | Minimum |
|---------|---------|
| Screen | 120 px wide |
| Print | 1.25 in wide |

Below these sizes, use the **lettermark** rather than shrinking the full logo.

### Placement

- Default position: top-left of a layout, or centered for closing/title frames.
- On photography or video, use the reversed mark over a sufficiently dark area —
  target at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the underlying image.
- The site applies a `bg-black/60` scrim over hero video before placing the
  reversed mark; match that treatment for equivalent contexts.

### Misuse — do not

- Stretch, condense, rotate, skew, or apply effects (shadows, glows, bevels, outlines).
- Recolor the mark. It is Royal `#002F6C`, black, or white — nothing else.
- Place the primary (dark) logo on a dark or busy background.
- Set the firm name in a substitute typeface in place of the logo.
- Add a tagline lockup, descriptor, or department name beneath the logo.
- Lock the logo to another organization's mark without written approval.
- Crop, enclose in a shape, or use as a repeating pattern.

## 6. Typography

Two families, deliberately paired: a clean sans for structure, a warm serif for
voice. Together they read institutional without feeling coldly technical.

### Lato — primary

Body copy, navigation, interface, data, and labels.

| Weight | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Light 300** | Large display copy and statement lines only |
| **Regular 400** | Body copy and all primary reading text |
| **Bold 700** | Emphasis, data labels, eyebrow labels |

```css
font-family: "Lato", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
```

> **Readability rule:** Light 300 is for display sizes only. Never use it for
> reading text — it was previously used for body copy and proved too thin.

### Unna — secondary display

Headlines, section titles, pull quotes, and editorial headlines — where a warmer,
more institutional voice is called for.

| Weight | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Regular 400** | Headlines, section titles, pull quotes |
| **Bold 700** | Occasional emphasis in editorial headings |
| *Italic 400* | Pull quotes and editorial emphasis |

```css
font-family: "Unna", Georgia, serif;
```

**Never** use Unna for body copy, UI, or data.

### Sourcing & fallbacks

Both families are open source, available from **Google Fonts**
(fonts.google.com/specimen/Lato, fonts.google.com/specimen/Unna). The website
self-hosts them via `next/font`.

| Primary | Fallback |
|---------|----------|
| Lato | Helvetica, Arial |
| Unna | Georgia |

### Type rules

- **Eyebrow labels:** Lato Bold 700, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.2em`, ~12px,
  in Electric Blue. This is a signature element — use it to open sections.
- **Headlines:** Unna Regular 400, sentence case, tight leading (~1.08).
- **Body:** Lato Regular 400, generous leading (1.6–1.75).
- **No orphan words.** Never strand a single word on its own line in a heading.
  Use `text-wrap: balance` in digital; adjust manually in print. Do not insert
  manual line breaks to fix it unless intentional.
- Sentence case for headings. Avoid all-caps except eyebrow labels.

## 7. Layout & design principles

### Design principles

1. **Minimal** — remove everything nonessential.
2. **Functional** — every element serves the content.
3. **Data-first** — charts are primary; type supports them.
4. **Professional** — trustworthy, precise, institutional.
5. **Consistent** — same palette, weights, and spacing throughout.

### Layout system

- **Structure through color and whitespace, not borders.** Sections separate by
  ground color (white ↔ `#f7f8fa`), not hard rules. Minimize visible lines —
  hairlines are reserved for intentional moments (e.g. dividers between figures).
- **Generous whitespace.** Clarity over density.
- **Grid:** modular and consistent; content column max ~1280px on web,
  ~720px for reading text.
- **Presentation aspect ratio:** 16:9.
- **Imagery breaks density.** Alternate text-led and image-led sections; never
  stack more than two text-only sections consecutively.

### Signature patterns

- **Eyebrow → serif headline → sans body.** The core section rhythm.
- **Figures band:** large Royal serif numerals with a raised superscript symbol
  (`$`, `#`), thin vertical hairline dividers, label beneath, single attributed
  source note.
- **Closing statement:** navy ground, centered QCC lettermark, "Own the outcome."
  in Lato Light Electric Blue, hairline rule, supporting line beneath.

## 8. Photography & imagery

### Direction

Imagery should depict **precision, permanence, and connected systems** — the
infrastructure the firm builds and its role as a bridge between scientific
discovery and real-world impact.

**Preferred subjects**

- Real people, real rooms: the team, panels, summits, working sessions.
- Authentic environments — labs, institutions, civic and campus settings.
- Coastal and horizon imagery consistent with the "coastal horizon" metaphor.
- Architectural and infrastructural forms; arches and horizon lines.

**Avoid**

- Stock "quantum" clichés: glowing orbs, abstract particle fields, blue circuit
  boards, hovering holograms.
- Generic business stock photography (handshakes, boardroom clichés).
- AI-generated imagery of people.
- Heavy filters or unnatural color grading.

### Treatment

- Video and photographic heroes carry a scrim for legibility:
  `bg-black/60` for neutral, or `bg-qcc-blue/70–80` for a brand-tinted ground.
- Corner radius: `rounded-lg` (8px) for cards, `rounded-xl` (12px) for feature
  imagery. Never fully rounded or circular crops for editorial photography.
- Uniform aspect ratios within any grid — 16:10 for cards, 4:5 for portraits,
  16:9 for feature imagery.

### Optimization (required)

All imagery must be optimized before publishing.

| Asset | Target |
|-------|--------|
| Images | WebP, quality ~80, max width 1600px |
| Portraits | WebP, 4:5 crop, max width 1200px |
| Video | H.264 MP4, 720p, audio stripped, `-movflags +faststart` |
| Video poster | WebP still frame, always supplied |

Reference: the homepage hero video was reduced from 28 MB to 1.2 MB with no
perceptible quality loss.
